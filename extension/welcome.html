🎉 Welcome to GSC Full Fetch & Render

It shows the whole page Google rendered in URL Inspection, not just the first 1,750 px of its screenshot, and lists every link on it with filters and a CSV download.

Pin it to the toolbar

Click the “Extensions” puzzle icon in the top right of Chrome

Then click the pin next to “GSC Full Fetch & Render as Googlebot by Natzir” so it turns blue

See a full page

  1. In Search Console, inspect a URL.
  2. Open View tested page or View crawled page.
  3. Go to the Screenshot tab. The whole page is there, and ⤢ opens it larger with its list of links.

It turns on by itself in URL Inspection. Click the icon to turn it off in that tab, and click it again to turn it back on. If you'd rather turn it on yourself each time, right-click the icon and uncheck “Turn on automatically in URL Inspection”.
